# Odins.ai

Odins is a managed marketing investment platform built on Bayesian marketing mix modeling (MMM). It helps companies answer three questions about their marketing: is the budget the right size, is it going to the right places, and how should it change next month.

You get software and a team that operates it. Odins handles the data integration, builds and maintains the models, and reviews every recommendation before it reaches you. It fits companies with a marketing budget above EUR 1M a year and a sales signal the model can learn from.

Companies like CDON, Nettbil, Hyre and Megaflis run their marketing budgets on Odins.

## What the platform does

- Collects your marketing data in one hierarchy, online and offline together: search, social, display, affiliates, TV, radio, out-of-home, influencers and sponsorship, alongside your sales data. See the [connectors](https://odins.ai/product/data-integration/connectors).
- Models what drives sales with a Bayesian model: baseline, adstock, saturation and business drivers such as pricing. Every estimate comes with a confidence range. See [the model](https://odins.ai/how-it-works/the-model).
- Lets you run scenarios and forecasts whenever you need them, and compare budgets before you commit. See the [scenario planner](https://odins.ai/product/scenario-planner).
- Delivers monthly recommendations on where to invest more and where to pull back, reviewed by a person. Where the data cannot settle a question, Odins proposes a structured test. See [recommendations and testing](https://odins.ai/product/recommendations-and-testing).
- Checks its own forecasts against what happened, using forecasts made before the actuals arrived. See [staying accurate](https://odins.ai/how-it-works/staying-accurate).

## Use Odins from Claude, ChatGPT or Copilot

The Odins MCP server connects your model to the AI assistant you already use. Ask how large next quarter's budget should be, what happens to sales if you move spend from one channel to another, or which channels return the most. Each answer comes with a number and a confidence range.

- [Odins MCP](https://odins.ai/product/odins-mcp)
- [Using marketing mix modelling from ChatGPT or Claude](https://odins.ai/guides/marketing-mix-modelling-chatgpt-claude)

The MCP server is part of the Odins platform and requires a subscription. It recommends, and you decide: it does not change anything in your ad accounts.

## Odins and the open-source frameworks

Google Meridian, Meta Robyn and PyMC-Marketing are free and capable frameworks for teams that have data scientists to run them. Odins builds on the same statistical principles and operates the whole thing as a product: data collection, prior setting, monthly retraining and review.
